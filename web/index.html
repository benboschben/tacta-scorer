Scores

    For an accurate count

    • Hold the phone directly above the table, not at an angle.
    • Use even light and avoid glare on the cards.
    • Get every card in the frame.
    • Remove any illegally placed cards first. The scorer can't spot them.

    Your photo never leaves your device. Scoring runs in your browser, and the first visit downloads about 20 MB to do it.
